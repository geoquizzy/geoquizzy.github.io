Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who we are

GeoQuizzy publishes short geography quiz videos. "GeoQuizzy Uploader" is a private desktop tool that the GeoQuizzy team uses to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel.

Use of YouTube API Services

GeoQuizzy Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • The app asks for permission to upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel (scope: youtube.upload).
  • It sends the video file, title, description and tags that we created to YouTube, and receives the ID of the uploaded video.
  • It does not read, collect or store any information about viewers or any other YouTube users.

How data is stored

The sign-in permission (an access token) and a log of uploaded video IDs are stored only on the GeoQuizzy team's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google account permissions page. Deleting the saved token file on the computer also removes the app's access.

Videos and comments

When you watch or comment on our videos on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, your data is handled by those platforms under their own privacy policies. GeoQuizzy does not collect it.
